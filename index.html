01About Us

One-Stop Decoration
Engineering Solutions

Shanghai Shengkai Builders Co., Ltd. was established in 2021. Our company has various statutory business licenses and A-level building registration certificates in the Philippines. Our professional services include financial institutions, factory offices, commercial spaces, real estate, and home services; there are also successful cases in various fields such as catering, entertainment gyms, and beauty salons.

The service content covers business certificate processing, storefront coordination, interior design, decoration, supervision, furniture and equipment. Innovation, integrity, and attitude are the corporate spirit of Shanghai Shengkai Builders. At the same time, we are committed to pursuing the three concepts of communication, design, and quality practice. Shanghai Shengkai Builders directly assigns its own design and engineering project management personnel to create a one-stop worry-free decoration project solution for customers, with providing the highest quality service as the first priority.

02Strength

Six Core Performances

We protect your life with our strength.

Silent Doors & Windows

Super-large insulating glass system, multi-cavity profile structure and a good sealing system effectively block outside noise for a quiet, comfortable home.

Strong Doors & Windows

High-performance Ti-Mg-Al alloy profile with thickened, strengthened frame structure — effectively resists high-rise wind pressure and typhoon weather.

Airtight Doors & Windows

Multi-sealing structure matched with automobile-grade EPDM sealing strips and an excellent sealing cavity ensures good air tightness of the product.

Energy-Saving Doors & Windows

Multi-cavity structure design, high-performance sealing system and insulating glass effectively block heat transfer and improve thermal insulation.

Waterproof Doors & Windows

Independent drainage design, multi-channel sealing structure, glue injection and angle-code technology — effectively preventing water seepage in heavy rain.

Security Doors & Windows

Partnering with Stanford, Jishi and other international hardware brands to solve security risks at the source, with standardized installation and inspection.
